Friday 0.4.0

12 November 2026 · Download

Friday plans the to-dos from the apps you already use, asks what you'll do in an event's time, and every click answers at once.

New

  • Todoist, TickTick, Notion, Trello, Apple Reminders, Things and Google Tasks: their to-dos are planned into your week, and ticking one off in either place ticks it off in both.
  • Asana, Linear, Jira and GitHub: work assigned to you is planned in. Canvas's own assignments, and Slack messages that mention you.
  • More calendar links from school and work, each with its steps: Blackboard, Moodle, Brightspace, Schoology, Homebase, When I Work, Deputy, 7shifts and Sling.
  • Mail from iCloud, Yahoo, Fastmail or any other provider, with an app password, beside Gmail.
  • Your plan as a "Friday" calendar in iCloud, so your iPhone shows it.
  • An event's card asks what you'll do: go, work through it (Friday plans work in its time), or skip it.
  • The first time Friday opens, a short tour shows each page and what it's for; afterwards the ? numbers the buttons on the page you're on and lists what each does.
  • friday:// links for Shortcuts: add a to-do or open a page.

Changed

  • Clicks answer at once: Inbox answers, timers and the length steppers no longer wait for Friday to finish saving.
  • Notes about you are on the Rules page, beside your rules: type one and press Keep as a note.
  • A block's card stays open after what you do in it, showing what changed.
  • Each integration opens in a box of its own, closed with × or Esc.
  • Settings says less, and more plainly.

Fixed

  • The Inbox no longer freezes when it opens before Friday has finished loading.
  • Enter in search, with nothing found, adds what you typed as a to-do.
  • A project's step shows what it waits on.
  • The weekly review offers Next week only for work due after next Monday.
  • A task due at midnight repeats on the right days.
  • What changed opens after you undo an add.
  • ⌘-clicking a second block picks the one you'd selected too.
  • Change a Setup answer opens the question you picked.
  • "today" typed after midnight means the same day as the Today button.
  • The to-do list's done section is called Done.
  • Double clicks no longer make two exams, projects, steps or to-dos, or send a review twice.
  • One integration that can't be read no longer stops the others from syncing.
  • Friday left open overnight moves on to the new day by itself.
  • The Mac app starts Friday again if it stops by itself, and no longer keeps the processor busy after it stops.
  • An event moved by an hour in Calendar or Reminders is synced at once, not up to an hour later.
  • Work due at exactly the start of a day is planned the day before.
  • Renaming a tag keeps the rules that name it.
  • Forgetting one mailbox no longer switches off another whose address ends the same.

Friday 0.3.3

10 November 2026

Connecting Gmail is right in Setup, and Settings no longer makes Google look calendar-only.

New

  • Setup lists Gmail beside your calendars: make an app password, paste it with your address, done.

Changed

  • Settings → Integrations keeps the advanced Google option (your own Google Cloud key) closed, and says most people don't need it.

Fixed

  • Connecting Gmail with an app password no longer opens the Google Cloud key-file steps.

Friday 0.3.2

9 November 2026

Google works for everyone through your Mac, Friday's questions wait in your Inbox, new terms to accept, and fixes for timers, time steppers and calendar edge cases.

New

  • Friday's terms: everyone accepts them when Friday opens, and again whenever they change.
  • Friday's questions now wait in your Inbox: what it learned, which version of a day you'd rather have, your meal times and your bedtimes. "Not now" asks again once Friday knows more; Insights links there.
  • When something from your mail updates a task you have, the card says what will change.

Changed

  • Google comes in through your Mac: add your Google account in System Settings → Internet Accounts for its calendars, and read Gmail with an app password. Signing in to Google directly, which Google blocked for most people, is now only for someone with a Google Cloud key of their own (Settings → Integrations → Advanced).
  • Friday's code is under the PolyForm Noncommercial License.

Fixed

  • "Forgot to stop?" starts at the time it is now, not 9am, and its buttons line up.
  • Tapping − and + quickly, or going back after overshooting, keeps the length you see.
  • Updating a task from your Inbox keeps the name you gave it.
  • A 2:30am event on the night the clocks go forward is no longer refused.
  • Changing your timezone keeps all-day events on their days.
  • An event made all-day in Google while all-day events are off leaves Friday.
  • Undoing a delete, or opening a deleted event again, puts it back on its calendar.
  • Work can't be logged before it has happened.
  • Security updates for the parts Friday is built with.

Friday 0.3.1

9 November 2026

Repeats you make work as they should, any Gmail with an app password, colours for events from the start, Friday on your phone, and many fixes from a full check of the app.

New

  • Friday on your phone over Tailscale: pair it with a QR code in Settings → Phone.
  • Read any Gmail with an app password, in Settings → Integrations → Gmail.
  • Events get colours from the start: Exam, Class, Meeting, Work, Exercise, Social and Travel, all yours to change.
  • The calendars on your Mac lead Settings → Integrations, and you can change one Setup answer without going through Setup again.
  • Keep a repeating task's times apart: "laundry at least 7 days apart".
  • A built-in preference to stay on one course for a stretch, and a switch for doing things in one go (Settings → Schedule).
  • A sentence with several times, like "no work 7:10–7:20 and 7:30–7:40", adds a rule for each; under any rule Friday read itself, "Not what you meant?" asks your AI.
  • "If math homework becomes overdue, it must be done before Sunday" is read as a rule.

Changed

  • Sleep and Meals are tabs of Insights.
  • Errors say what happened and what to do, in plain words.
  • The intro's town matches the season outside.
  • Double-clicking a planned task opens it in your to-dos.
  • Many more rules are read without the AI, and one Friday only half understands goes to the AI instead of being guessed.

Fixed

  • Events you make in Friday repeat as they should.
  • Switching off a repeat that came from Google no longer deletes it from Google.
  • Canvas and other calendar-link repeats with an end date come in, once, and aren't copied into Google.
  • Free/busy and all-day changes made in Google reach Friday.
  • Quick add reads "due 5pm fri", page ranges and times after midnight as meant, and exam prep by its title.
  • Work you've already timed today counts toward your daily limit; changing a task's length changes what's planned.
  • Security: other websites and connected assistants can no longer reach your AI key or your private settings.
  • Security updates for Google sign-in.

Friday 0.3.0

8 November 2026

Friday now lives at friday-scheduler.com, asks you less about your day, and gets birthdays and all-day events right.

New

  • Friday's site is friday-scheduler.com: the download, the Terminal install and updates all come from there, and downloads come from servers near you, so they're quicker.

Changed

  • With "Plan it again" on (Settings → Schedule), Friday no longer asks how much you did of work you didn't time: it already knows.
  • A timer stopped in under five minutes doesn't count as work: Friday says it was too short to count and keeps the task to do.
  • A new all-day event, like a birthday, leaves the day free for planning unless you say otherwise.

Fixed

  • Asking the AI to add a list of birthdays puts each one on the right day, whichever AI you use, and doesn't add one you already have.
  • Changing a repeating event from today on keeps its next date: yearly birthdays no longer all moved to today.
  • Deleting a repeating event from one time on removes those times from your calendar too.

Friday 0.2.0

7 November 2026

Friday now updates itself, asking you first and saying what changed.

New

  • When a new version is out, Friday asks the next time it opens, with what's new in it.
  • Update downloads it while you keep working, checks it's the version Friday's site published, and restarts into it.
  • The first time a new version opens, Friday shows what changed.
  • Settings → Updates: the version you have, Check now, and whether an update goes in right away or when you quit.

Friday 0.1.0

6 November 2026

The first version of Friday you can download.

New

  • Plans your week: tasks planned into your free time before they’re due, around your classes, work, meals and sleep, and planned again when things change.
  • Walk through your week: a first run through a pixel-art town that sets Friday up from your answers.
  • One list: everything you have to do by kind, with its time, when it’s planned and when it’s due.
  • Your Mac’s calendars and calendar feeds come in; events you make can go back to your calendar.
  • The Inbox, the review and rules in your own words: Friday asks only what it can’t know.
  • Ask the AI you choose, or none.
  • Everything runs on your Mac: no account, nothing to install besides the app.
