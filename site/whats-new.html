What's new

Every version of Friday, newest first: what's new, and what changed.

Friday 0.6.7

23 November 2026 · Download

Friday works as it did in 0.6.5 again: 0.6.6 broke things that worked before, so its changes are taken out until they're fixed.

Changed

  • Taken out until they're fixed, as before 0.6.6: renaming a task from its name in the To-do details, the step-by-step Insights → This week, Insights' count of the timed tasks estimates are learned from (learned the night after again), and skipping the replan when a change leaves your plan as it was.

Fixed

  • What 0.6.6 broke works as it did in 0.6.5.

Friday 0.6.6

22 November 2026

Rename a task from its name, an Inbox that keeps up when you answer quickly, and estimates learned as soon as you've timed enough.

New

  • Click a task's name at the top of its details on the To-do page to rename it. Enter saves; Escape leaves it as it was.

Changed

  • Insights → This week says what it's for and what to do, step by step, instead of marking the tab "suggested".
  • Learned estimates: Insights counts the timed tasks Friday learns from and says how many don't count (timed on a break or at another pace). Friday learns from them as soon as there are five, not the night after.
  • A change that leaves your plan as it was, like turning on estimate adjustment before anything is learned, no longer replans.

Fixed

  • Start or Resume showed what was planned in the Now bar until Friday answered, so it could look like the wrong task had started. The bar now shows the task you started at once.
  • Accepting Inbox suggestions quickly could leave them looking unanswered, bring them back after you left the Inbox, and then say they weren't waiting. They now stay answered.
  • In the week view with a break and the To-do list open, the break's name wrapped over several lines and pushed the calendar down. It now takes one line.

Friday 0.6.5

22 November 2026

Fixes from your notes: what you're doing at once shows side by side, breaks leave your errands alone, and a sleep-in or a quick delete no longer leaves things behind.

New

  • Breaks: repeating tasks, like errands and chores, aren't planned on break days unless you say so, in Settings → Breaks or in the break itself.

Changed

  • The Now bar's minimal style shows everything you're timing, and a meal you're eating, side by side, each with its own time.
  • Timing work that's also planned next, the Now bar says how long it has left in all, then the next other thing.
  • The question about when you do work with no due date leaves out a project's steps: they're due with their project.

Fixed

  • An event deleted right after you made it could still appear on your calendar. It's now deleted there too.
  • Up later than planned, what the plan had while you slept or got ready stayed on the day, and could count as done. It now goes, and is planned again.
  • On a break, a daily errand was late all through an Off break and on every Light day's list.
  • A test user's Mac could be listed twice in Friday's list of test devices after joining with an invite.

Friday 0.6.4

21 November 2026

Breaks: take days off, or light days with a short list, and Friday plans around them. Replanning is much faster, and many fixes from your notes.

New

  • Breaks: drag across days on the calendar, or use the Breaks button at the top, to make days a break, as long as you like.
  • An Off break has nothing to do: work due during it is planned before it starts. A Light break holds about the time a day you choose, as a short list of tasks by name, each with a tick and a timer.
  • On break days your repeating events pause (classes, shifts, meetings) while one-off events stay; meals and sleep aren't planned unless you say so; tasks with a tag you pause wait until it's over.
  • Friday suggests breaks in the Inbox from your calendar: all-day events that say you're away (vacation, out of office, spring break and more), and weekdays where your repeating events stop. Each break shows as one all-day event on Friday's own calendar, so your phone shows it too.
  • Work that's pointless once late (an exam's prep, or a step you marked Pointless) is archived once its due passes. The Inbox says what went and why, with Bring back and OK.
  • When work won't make it, Friday can also offer staying up an hour later tonight, getting up an hour earlier tomorrow, or giving less important work one more day. Each is tried on a copy of your plan first.
  • New exam asks how many days before it to plan only its class's work, or follows your rule in Rules for an exam of that name.
  • Test users join with an invite link, which joins the device it's opened on, or by pasting it in Settings → Updates.

Changed

  • Replanning is much faster: a busy week that took minutes to plan takes seconds, and What if plans your week as it opens, so a change you try is answered sooner. A newer try stops the one still being worked out.
  • Every dropdown is Friday's own menu. The calendar lists show each calendar with its color and account.
  • Settings → Breaks holds your defaults: what pauses, the time a light day holds, meals and sleep. The Breaks button takes the place of the Inbox's tray at the top of the plan; the Inbox keeps its count in the sidebar.
  • The buttons at the top of the plan fold into the Plan menu as the window gets narrow. On a phone, What if's bar has a line of its own, with Discard and Keep in reach.
  • When nothing Friday tries would help late work, the window says so and where to go, instead of an empty list.
  • Insights' "When you get things done" counts every hour: work before 8am or after midnight shows, after midnight on the evening it belongs to.
  • A finished step fills a project's bar to its diamond, however quickly you finished it. The sidebar's day leaves out events you said you're not going to, as the calendar does.

Fixed

  • Two tasks with the same name for different classes (HW 12 for CHEM and for MATH), or due days apart, were taken as copies of one. Copies are now the same words, the same tags and dues within a day.
  • Friday offered to plan a meal shorter than it said it takes you ("about 20m", then "Plan 15m"). It now offers at least what it takes, to the quarter hour.
  • A What if that took long could end with the browser's "couldn't parse" error. A cut-off answer now says so plainly.
  • "Replanning…" could sit over the Day and 3 days buttons. The header now makes room for it.
  • Steps after one with no room before its due were planned anyway. They now wait for it and say why.
  • An exam whose prep was planned to finish before it said "Prep runs past the exam" when some prep didn't fit. It now says how much doesn't fit and when the rest finishes.
  • A long menu, like New exam's or the Breaks list, ran past its edge or jumped back to its top; it now scrolls.
  • A short block's time was cut mid-letter in a narrow column, and a long to-do list squeezed its rows; both now fit.
  • Dragging across days to make a break selected the text on the page as well.

Friday 0.5.5

19 November 2026

Friday starts even when another app uses its port, exam focus is set for each exam, and fewer, better-timed questions.

New

  • Exam focus is set for each exam in its popup: how many days before it only its class's work is planned, or on the exam's own day until it starts. An exam without its own follows your rule in Rules.

Changed

  • A project's steps say on each step which one it comes after ("after Outline"), or "doesn't wait".
  • Friday looks for a new version when it opens, once a day, and when you come back to it after six hours or more, rather than every ten minutes.
  • A sleep tap you missed is asked about only once it's two hours past its usual time.
  • Adding an exam focus rule says which one it takes the place of, and Rules shows your own words under a rule Friday reads otherwise.

Fixed

  • When another app (Anki, for one) used Friday's port, Friday couldn't start. It now moves to a free port, and stops a copy of its own server that stopped answering.
  • An exam's late prep was offered a later due, which can't help once the exam is over. It's now offered events to skip instead, including ones you said you're going to.
  • Steps waiting for one with no room before its due said "No room before its due" themselves. They now say what they wait for, and the Inbox asks about them with it.
  • "On the day of a prelim, study until it starts" was read as the day before.
  • When you're usually ready to work ignored the morning routine Friday had learned.

Get new versions early as a test user, and What if no longer plans again what you've already started today.

New

Fixed

  • Settings → Updates said "Looked just now" every time when Friday's time zone was ahead of the Mac's. It now says when Friday really last looked.
  • After you chose Leave it late, Friday could ask about the same work again every time you added something. Your answer now stands for a day, whatever you add, and after that it asks only if the work has become an hour later.

Friday 0.5.1

17 November 2026

Projects open in a popup and list their steps on To-do, steps run in the order you give them, and work isn't left unplanned when there's time.

New

  • A project, an exam or an ongoing project opens in a popup in the middle of the screen. Escape, the ✕ or a click outside closes it.
  • A project with steps shows on To-do as a heading over its steps, not as a to-do of its own; click the heading to open the project. A project with no steps is still a to-do, worked on as a whole.
  • Ticking a project's last step finishes the project too, and one Undo puts both back.
  • If you gave a project more time than its steps add up to, the rest is a step of its own, "More on <project>", kept up to date as you change the steps. Delete it and it stays gone.
  • A project's steps run in the order you list them, each after the one above it. Click "then" between two steps to make one "any time" instead. Reordering moves them at once.
  • A project's due date and how firm it is are set at the top of the project.
  • An exam's popup says what happens before an exam, "Only its class's work, 3 days before", and you can change it right there for all your exams.
  • Every page says "Replanning…" while Friday plans a change, then "Plan updated".
  • An event's color can be changed on its card.
  • If you're not in school, New project offers a report, a presentation and an event to plan.

Changed

  • A project's pace (front-loaded, evenly or toward the end) now visibly changes when its work is planned.
  • A step planned past the next two weeks says when ("Later, by Wed 9 Dec") instead of "not planned".
  • Projects & exams lists only projects and exams; big assignments stay on To-do.
  • An ongoing project can have a tag.
  • "Focus on exams starting 5 days before" and similar wording are understood as exam focus.
  • A due date you pick is said as a sentence, "Due Tue 24 Nov, 11:59pm (in 7 days)".
  • Try elsewhere, when nothing else fits, says what rules the other times out.
  • Time you shoved says what it is on its card.

Fixed

  • Work could be left unplanned, even with free time, when that time came only in short gaps (an ongoing project could get no time at all that week).
  • A daily repeat set to be spaced several days apart stopped Friday from planning.
  • A timer started before midnight shows on the previous day too.
  • Steps that all had lengths were told to "give them a length".
  • Steps with similar names were made to wait on each other.
  • A rule clash in the Inbox could say work moves "to 10:15am from 10:15am".
  • An event's location could run under a meal or a timer drawn over it.
  • Hints in typing boxes were cut off in a few places, and a Morning brief line on a phone.
  • The Now bar's timing dot was cut off on its left.
  • An overdue due date said "in -1 days"; it says "yesterday" or "3 days ago".
  • A rule clash could be reported when keeping the rule gained almost nothing.

Friday 0.5.0

16 November 2026

A new Projects & exams page with ongoing projects, choices for events marked free, and lengths you can type.

New

  • Projects & exams is a new page: every deadline (projects, exams and big assignments) as a row across the next six weeks, your ongoing projects, and what you've finished. Everything you had before is on it.
  • Ongoing projects: hours a week with no deadline, like guitar or a side project. Choose how many days a week, its priority, a pause, and whether a short week carries into the next. Weekly goals you already had are listed there and plan as they always have until you change a setting.
  • A project can be archived when it's done, keep notes, finish a few days early, set its pace through the work (front-loaded, even or toward the end), and have milestones.
  • An exam opens beside the page, its prep a list you edit: rename, length, by when, tick, delete, or add more.
  • Events marked free in your calendar have the same choices as busy ones: I'm going, Work through it and Skip it. Work through it is chosen until you pick.

Changed

  • A length (an exam's prep, a project or step, hours a week) offers common amounts and a box to type any, up to 1,000 hours.
  • Double-click a task's name in the To-do drawer to rename it.
  • Everything you're timing shows on the calendar while it runs, side by side.
  • Starting a nap while something is being timed asks whether to stop timing it.
  • The Now bar shows an event that takes your time before a free one that began earlier.
  • A pace set for a while says the day it ends in words: "Easy pace until tomorrow".
  • In the Inbox, the email a suggestion came from opens at the words Friday read.

Fixed

  • Insights' hours map counts an hour once however many timers ran in it, and says it covers the weeks shown.
  • Two quick changes to the same thing are saved in the order you made them.
  • A dropdown's list is as wide as its button, not the whole window.
  • The Inbox's messages keep their height in a long list, and the plan's notice keeps its count on a phone.

Friday 0.4.2

15 November 2026

All-day events and holidays come in from your calendars, rule clashes say why, the week can be lightened in a click, and the calendar turns over at midnight.

New

  • All-day events from your calendars, like holidays and your own all-day plans, show at the top of each day. One that reads like homework becomes a to-do due that day.
  • A rule clash says why, e.g. "finishing Lab 9 2 days sooner splits it into 3 sittings instead of 1". Only clashes where your answer would change the plan wait in the Inbox.
  • When a week or a day holds more than usual, "Lighten that week" offers work that can move later without being late, each tried first and undoable.
  • Change a project's or an exam's tag from its page or card. Its steps that had the old tag follow.
  • Remove a template you saved with the ✕ on its card, and saving one under a name you've used asks before replacing it.
  • A new exam takes any length: 45m to 3h in a click, or type one like 2h 30m.

Changed

  • The calendar turns over at midnight: anything after midnight shows under the next date, and a block that crosses midnight shows in both days.
  • An event that doesn't block your time sits beside the work planned over it, not hidden behind it.
  • The two rules for meals and routines say what they're set to, like "Give meals and routines their ideal length: Lunch 45 min and Dinner 1 h".

  • The Minimal Now bar is a one-line pill; tap it for the full bar.
  • Text in a tall block wraps instead of being cut off.
  • A repeat says its spacing as a sentence: "Every week, at least 5 days after the last time".
  • "Final" marks an exam ("math final") for students, but not a final project, paper or round, or a name like Final Fantasy.
  • The Rules lab is hidden while it's unfinished; Settings → Advanced shows it again.

Fixed

  • The Now bar no longer covers the Inbox's Accept and Decline (or any page's last buttons) when the sleep and meal rows are up.
  • "At least N days apart" now also spaces several times in the same week.
  • Save as template no longer keeps steps you deleted.
  • Load ahead no longer says a week is over your usual hours when it isn't: meals, goals and this morning's work were counted against it.
  • Rules no longer says a rule wins over one you've removed.
  • The day comparison runs from your day start, not midnight.
  • The Now bar's time no longer gets cut off in a narrow window.

Friday 0.4.1

15 November 2026

Edit a rule where it stands and see why one wasn't kept, stop work for the night with one tap, choose which Reminders lists Friday plans, and see what's due in a row under each date.

New

  • Edit a rule instead of deleting it: the pencil opens it with your words. Saving keeps its place and history, and Undo puts it back.
  • A rule the plan couldn't keep says "Violated on Tue, Thu", and Why? says in plain words what needed those hours.
  • Exam focus is a rule now ("Focus on an exam's course 5 days before it"), listed in Rules with its switch and its days to change.
  • Exam prep is spread over the days before the exam by default, as a rule in Rules you can switch off or make stronger.
  • Apple Reminders: choose which of your lists come into Friday. A list you switch off leaves your plan at once and stays in Reminders.
  • A 24-hour clock, in Settings → Appearance → Clock: every page, Siri and the command line follow it.
  • A small spinner on an integration while Friday reads it for the first time, and reads now start within a second.

Changed

  • After "Done with work", nothing more is planned that night. What then can't be done on time waits in the Inbox.
  • A calendar you hid is ignored everywhere: its invitations no longer come to the Inbox.
  • The sleep buttons show when you need them: Done with work from 6pm, Going to bed right after it, and I'm up after any night you went to bed.
  • An optional early event you haven't said you're going to no longer wakes you early.
  • Tasks are no longer marked spread or clustered. Say it as a rule instead, like "spread my essays over several days".
  • The Rules box has no row of examples under it.
  • The to-do lane is a row under each day's date with what's due that day: late ones first, three and "+N more" for the rest. The hours below keep their full width.
  • The Setup walk's times are in a clearer pixel font: 6:15 no longer reads as 6:18, nor 12am as 18am.
  • A project's step says which project it's in, on its card and in the to-do list, and opens it.
  • Colors are listed in rainbow order, gray last.
  • Friday is written in US English: color, gray.

Fixed

  • Going to bed at 4am or later counts as the night before, so the morning after pairs with it.
  • The red line for now shows at night and before you wake, not only during your day.
  • Plans no longer leave a long empty gap late at night between pieces of work, and replanning can improve the plan you have.
  • Text stays inside its box across the app, the calendars table fits its panel, and the "Read events as" menus are smaller.
  • A project whose steps are planned is no longer said to be unplanned, and once its steps are done its own time isn't planned.
  • A rule's hours before or after a time run to the start of your day, not 4am.
  • Stopping and restarting a repeat keeps the times you removed one by one.
  • Removing a source and forgetting it keeps the suggestions you'd accepted from it.
  • Work due at midnight on a Saturday isn't planned a week late.
  • Meals and naps across a clock change keep their times and lengths.
  • Signing in to Slack, Microsoft and others no longer runs out when two parts of Friday renew it at once.
  • A Shortcut that adds "Tom & Jerry" adds all of it, & and # included.
  • The mini calendar follows the calendar, and the View buttons keep the day you're looking at.
  • The paint menu closes with Esc, and an integration's box keeps the keyboard inside it.
  • What if and other slower questions no longer hold up the rest of the page.
  • Setup's day bars keep their times inside the box, and "fits" no longer reads as "Ats".

Friday 0.3.0

8 November 2026

Friday now lives at friday-scheduler.com, asks you less about your day, and gets birthdays and all-day events right.

New

  • Friday's site is friday-scheduler.com: the download, the Terminal install and updates all come from there, and downloads come from servers near you, so they're quicker.

Changed

  • With "Plan it again" on (Settings → Schedule), Friday no longer asks how much you did of work you didn't time: it already knows.
  • A timer stopped in under five minutes doesn't count as work: Friday says it was too short to count and keeps the task to do.
  • A new all-day event, like a birthday, leaves the day free for planning unless you say otherwise.

Fixed

  • Asking the AI to add a list of birthdays puts each one on the right day, whichever AI you use, and doesn't add one you already have.
  • Changing a repeating event from today on keeps its next date: yearly birthdays no longer all moved to today.
  • Deleting a repeating event from one time on removes those times from your calendar too.

Friday 0.2.0

7 November 2026

Friday now updates itself, asking you first and saying what changed.

New

  • When a new version is out, Friday asks the next time it opens, with what's new in it.
  • Update downloads it while you keep working, checks it's the version Friday's site published, and restarts into it.
  • The first time a new version opens, Friday shows what changed.
  • Settings → Updates: the version you have, Check now, and whether an update goes in right away or when you quit.

Friday 0.1.0

6 November 2026

The first version of Friday you can download.

New

  • Plans your week: tasks planned into your free time before they’re due, around your classes, work, meals and sleep, and planned again when things change.
  • Walk through your week: a first run through a pixel-art town that sets Friday up from your answers.
  • One list: everything you have to do by kind, with its time, when it’s planned and when it’s due.
  • Your Mac’s calendars and calendar feeds come in; events you make can go back to your calendar.
  • The Inbox, the review and rules in your own words: Friday asks only what it can’t know.
  • Ask the AI you choose, or none.
  • Everything runs on your Mac: no account, nothing to install besides the app.
