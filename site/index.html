The first two minutes

Walk through your week once.

The first time you open Friday, you walk through a little town from waking up to going to bed. On the way it asks what only you know: when you get up, when you eat, when you're in class or at work.

By bedtime your first week is planned. Or skip the walk and keep Friday's guesses; you can change any of it later.

On your Mac

Your tasks, plans and calendars stay on your computer. There's no Friday server holding them.

No account

Nothing to sign up for. Download it, open it, walk through your week.

Free

Free to download and use, and open source soon so anyone can see what it does.
