Too much to do? Friday finds the time for it.

Tell Friday what you have to do and when it's due. It plans when you'll do each thing, around your classes, shifts, meals and sleep, and plans again whenever something changes.

Download for Mac

Free · Version 0.1.0 · 241 MB · macOS 14 or later on Apple silicon (M1 or newer)

Friday isn't signed by Apple yet, so the first time you open it macOS asks you to allow it. How to open Friday.

It plans

Every task gets a time

Readings, essays, problem sets, chores and errands land in the free time before they're due, sized to how long they take you.

It keeps up

Plans again when life happens

Ran long, skipped something, got a new deadline? Friday moves what has to move and leaves the rest where it was.

It's yours

On your computer, not ours

Friday runs on your Mac. Your plans, tasks and calendars stay there. No account, nothing to sign up for.

Getting started

Walk through your week once

The first time you open Friday, you walk through a town from waking up to going to bed. A few questions on the way (when you get up, when you eat, when you're in class or at work) and Friday plans your first week around your answers. About two minutes, or skip it and keep Friday's guesses.

Your list

One list, already in order

Everything you have to do, grouped by kind, with how long it takes, when Friday plans it and when it's due. What's late is marked, and what has stopped mattering fades instead of nagging you.
