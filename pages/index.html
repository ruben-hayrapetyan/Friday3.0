Your first two minutes

Set up by taking a walk.

The first time you open Friday, you walk through a little town from waking up to going to bed. On the way it asks what only you know: when you get up, when you eat, when you're in class or at work.

By bedtime, your first week is planned. In a hurry? Skip the walk and change anything later.

Private by design

Your week stays on your Mac.

Friday isn't a website you log in to. It's an app that runs on your computer, and your plans live there.

Questions

Is Friday really free?

Yes. There's no trial and no subscription. Friday will also be open source.

Why does my Mac warn me the first time I open it?

Friday isn't signed by Apple yet, so macOS asks you to allow it once: click Done, then Open Anyway in System Settings → Privacy & Security (step by step). Or install it with one line in Terminal, and there’s nothing to allow: how.

Does it need an AI?

No. Planning, replanning and everything else work without one. An AI only adds asking for changes in your own words.

Does it work with Google Calendar?

Your Mac's calendars and calendar links work now. Google Calendar and Gmail are coming to the download soon.

Is there a Windows, Linux or iPhone version?

Not yet. Windows and Linux versions are planned; Friday is a Mac app for now, for Macs with Apple silicon.

Where is my data?

In ~/Library/Application Support/Friday on your Mac, with a backup every day. Privacy.
