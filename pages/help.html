Help

Getting Friday

  1. Download Friday (a {{size}} disk image). It needs a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or newer) and macOS 14 or later.
  2. Open Friday-{{version}}.dmg from your Downloads, and drag Friday onto the Applications folder beside it.
  3. Open Friday from Applications.

The first time it opens

Friday walks you through a town from waking up to going to bed, with a few questions on the way: when you get up and go to bed, when you eat, when you're in class or at work. Your answers become your first week's plan. It takes about two minutes; you can skip it and keep Friday's guesses, and change anything later in Settings.

After that, a short tour shows you each page and what it's for: your plan, your to-dos, projects and exams, the Inbox, Insights, rules and Settings. Later, the ? beside Settings numbers the buttons on whatever page you're on and lists what each one does.

Adding what you have to do

Type it the way you'd say it in the box at the top of the To-do page: essay 3h due fri, read chapter 4 1h due tomorrow. Friday plans it into your week at once.

Your calendars

Friday asks to see your Mac's calendars the first time it opens; choose which ones it reads in Settings → Calendars. For Google Calendar, add your Google account to your Mac (System Settings → Internet Accounts) and it comes in with the rest. A calendar link from your school or work (an .ics link) is added in Settings → Integrations.

To have deadlines read from your mail, connect it in Settings → Integrations with an app password from your mail provider (Gmail, iCloud, Yahoo, Fastmail or another); the box there walks you through making one.

Connecting your other apps

Settings → Integrations has a box for each app Friday connects to: Todoist, TickTick, Notion, Trello, Apple Reminders, Things, Google Tasks, Asana, Linear, Jira, GitHub, Canvas and Slack. Open one and follow its steps: for some you sign in, for others you paste a token the box shows you how to make. Google Tasks connects through a short script in your own Google account; the box has it ready to copy, and says what to press when Google warns that it hasn't checked the script (it's yours, so that's expected). What comes in waits in your Inbox the first time, then goes straight into your plan.

Connecting an AI

In Settings → Integrations → AI, choose the AI you use: the claude command if you have it, an API key from Claude, OpenAI or Gemini, or a model running on your Mac. Without one, everything works except asking in words.

Where your data is

Everything Friday knows is in ~/Library/Application Support/Friday on your Mac, with a backup made every day in its backups folder. Nothing is kept anywhere else.

Updating

Friday tells you itself when there's a new version, and what's new in it: press Update, and it updates and opens again. You can also look in Settings → Updates. Your data stays where it is.

Removing Friday

Quit Friday, drag it from Applications to the Bin, and, if you want your data gone too, delete ~/Library/Application Support/Friday.
